# Education Intelligence & 5D Holistic Student Triage System

An AI-powered **Neuro-Pedagogical, Talent Discovery & Holistic Student Triage System** built for schools, educators, parents, and students.

## Overview

The system governs and supports students through **interactive stealth arcade assessments, micro-quizzes, and daily bio-psycho-social check-ins** across 5 dimensions:

1. **Cognitive & Brain Wiring**: Working memory, processing speed, impulse control (Go/No-Go), cognitive flexibility, and dominant learning modalities (Visual-Spatial, Auditory-Verbal, Kinesthetic-Experiential, Analytical-Systems).
2. **Intrinsic Talent Discovery**: Systems & engineering, quantitative logic, creative synthesis, linguistic narrative, interpersonal leadership, and kinesthetic rhythm.
3. **Biological, Nutritional & Adolescent Growth**: Sleep architecture, morning protein & hydration balance, glycemic crash risk, and adolescent pubertal/growth-spurt fatigue context.
4. **Psychological & Digital Wellness**: Stress resilience, frustration recovery, daily screen time, and late-night social media circadian impact.
5. **Socio-Cultural, Family & Religious Harmony**: Peer belonging, home study environment, family support sync, and cultural/spiritual schedule alignment.
