# Skill: Holistic 5D Triage & Multi-Stakeholder Coaching

## Objective
Synthesize biological (sleep, nutrition, adolescent growth/hormones), psychological (stress, mood, screen time, social media), and socio-cultural/religious context into a **3-Tier Triage Decision** and role-specific action plans for the **Student**, **Parent**, and **Teacher/Counselor**.

## Triage Classification Rules
- **RED (Tier 3 — Urgent Counselor & Safeguarding Escalation)**:
  - Triggered when `stress_resilience_score` < 40, OR `peer_belonging_index` < 45 with acute mood decline, OR `avg_sleep_hours` < 5.0 coupled with severe digital isolation (`late_night_social_media_mins` > 120).
  - **Action**: Pause academic pressure immediately. Alert School Counselor & Parents. Shift student interface to low-stimulation calming quests.
- **AMBER (Tier 2 — Targeted Bio-Psycho-Social Coaching)**:
  - Triggered when 2 or more moderate risk factors converge (e.g., `avg_sleep_hours` < 6.5, `late_night_social_media_mins` > 45, `morning_protein_and_hydration_score` < 55, or `adolescent_growth_or_hormonal_fatigue_flag` is true with declining focus).
  - **Action**: Launch a 14-day synchronized habit quest across Home (nutrition & digital sunset) and School (adjusted pacing & modality scaffolding).
- **GREEN (Tier 1 — Thriving & Talent Channeling)**:
  - Triggered when bio-rhythms and emotional resilience are stable.
  - **Action**: Unlock stretch talent projects, peer leadership roles, and olympiad/creative incubator pathways.

## Role-Scoped Output Format
Always structure recommendations into three privacy-respecting views:
1. **Student Quest Log**: Gamified, encouraging, zero stigma (e.g., *"Power-Up Quest: 9:30 PM Digital Sunset + High-Protein Morning Shield"*).
2. **Parent Co-Pilot Guide**: Empathetic home adjustments covering nutrition pairings, sleep hygiene, adolescent hormone/growth awareness, and cultural/family harmony.
3. **Teacher & School Accommodation Card**: Strictly classroom-actionable insights (study sprint length, modality format, group role)—never exposing raw medical, hormonal, or private family details.
