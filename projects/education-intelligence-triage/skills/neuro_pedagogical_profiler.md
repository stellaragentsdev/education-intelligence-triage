# Skill: Neuro-Pedagogical & Brain-Wiring Profiler

## Objective
Translate a student's stealth arcade game telemetry, executive function scores, and biological energy curves into a concrete, personalized **Learning Methodology Blueprint** and **Talent Acceleration Path**.

## Step-by-Step Analysis Protocol
1. **Map Cognitive Architecture**:
   - Inspect `working_memory_index`, `sustained_attention_stamina_mins`, `impulse_control_go_nogo_score`, and `cognitive_flexibility_score`.
   - Set **Optimal Study Sprint Duration** = `sustained_attention_stamina_mins` + 2 minutes (capped at 35 mins before a mandatory bio-break).
2. **Select Primary & Secondary Modality Scaffolds**:
   - **Visual-Spatial**: Prescribe 3D interactive sandboxes, dual-coding (sketch + keyword), color-coded concept graphs, and spatial memory palaces.
   - **Auditory-Verbal**: Prescribe Socratic voice/chat tutoring, rhythmic mnemonics, debate-style framing, and "Feynman peer-teaching" voice notes.
   - **Kinesthetic-Experiential**: Prescribe rapid arcade-style micro-challenges, physical manipulatives, standing/movement study sprints, and build-first-theory-second lessons.
   - **Analytical-Systems**: Prescribe first-principles causal trees, "break-the-system" debugging quests, and matrix comparisons.
3. **Channel Core Talents into Academic Subjects**:
   - Bridge weaker academic subjects through the student's **Top Talent Domain** (e.g., if a student has 94% *Systems & Engineering* talent but 61% *Linguistic/Narrative*, teach essay writing as "Modular System Architecture for Arguments").
